## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Assets provide marketplace loadout access
The BIS Assets experience SHALL provide access to management of owned Stealth & Steel equipment while preserving inspection and existing safeguards for all other assets.

#### Scenario: Player opens Assets with catalog and generic assets
- **WHEN** a player has both marketplace equipment and a generic Signet asset
- **THEN** Assets shows the generic asset without change and exposes loadout controls for the recognized equipment
