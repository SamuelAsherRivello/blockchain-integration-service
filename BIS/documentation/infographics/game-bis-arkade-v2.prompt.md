## Generation prompt

Create an entirely fresh, premium player-facing marketing infographic for a browser stealth game connected through BIS to the Arkade backend. Use case: infographic-diagram / gaming campaign artwork. This is version 2 but do not print version numbers on the artwork.

Creative direction: cinematic illustrated gaming campaign, sophisticated, thrilling, approachable. Portrait 4:5 composition at high resolution. A single cohesive visual world, not three columns, not a dashboard, not an engineering architecture diagram. Upper half dominated by beautiful stylized 3D stealth adventure artwork: an agile cloaked adventurer moving through atmospheric stone-and-steel maze corridors, avoiding a guard with a clear warm searchlight/vision cone, collecting small gold pickups and approaching an illuminated exit. A large glowing collectible trophy is the visual reward, with no financial-profit imagery. Teal shadows, deep ink blue, warm amber light, ivory typography; dynamic composition, clean generous typography zones. Conceptual artwork rather than a purported screenshot. No firearms, combat or violence.

Top headline, very large elegant condensed bold typography, exactly:
"STAY HIDDEN.
MAKE YOUR MARK."
Top supporting sentence exactly:
"A stealth adventure with Bitcoin-powered possibilities."

In the game scene, tastefully positioned editorial callout with ample contrast:
"THE STEALTH GAME"
"Your mission. Your moves."
"Outsmart guards. Collect gold. Find the exit."

Below the main illustration the environment elegantly reveals two connected support zones, like a cutaway showing what powers the adventure. BIS first, Arkade below it. Each gets substantial visual emphasis, a memorable icon illustration, and only the copy specified here. Show a clear flowing illuminated connection from THE STEALTH GAME to BIS, then from BIS to ARKADE. This is a simple player-readable relationship, no circuit-board spiderwebs or technical labels. Game visually uses BIS, BIS visually uses Arkade. No direct bypass from game to Arkade.

Middle support zone, in teal with a beautiful minimal player account card and collectible trophy emblem (no sample balances, no fake UI numbers):
"BIS"
"The connection to more."
"Brings your account, payments and trophy collection into the game."
Small attractive three-item strip:

Lower support zone, rich warm amber with a small radiant Bitcoin emblem, abstract distant network constellation and collectible token, no server racks:
"ARKADE"
"Bitcoin power, behind the scenes."
"Runs the shared network behind Bitcoin payments and digital collectibles."

Closing line, confident and highly readable:
"You focus on the adventure."

Discrete but clearly legible footer:
"Playable prototype • Signet test network • Test sats only"

Accuracy and simplicity: BIS stands for Blockchain Integration Service; spell its label BIS, never BS or BGS. Spell ARKADE, not Arcade. The diagram must show all three named parts and what each does using the exact minimal text above. The Game owns gameplay. BIS brings the account, payment and trophy experience to the game. Arkade is the shared backend network used through BIS. Do not include SDK, API, React, WebGPU, adapters, settlement, VTXO, backend code, charts, fees, implementation status checklists, or architecture jargon in the visible image. No claims of real-money earnings, free trophies, cross-game interoperability, guaranteed security, immediate settlement, or production readiness. Trophy collection is optional and may cost test sats; do not imply automatic or free rewards. The headline Bitcoin-powered refers to a clearly labeled Signet prototype. Retain the test-network footer. No official logo invention, use simple ARKADE wordmark text. Text must be exceptionally crisp, spelled correctly, not crowded, with distinct headline/body hierarchy. Make this something a player would want to look at, with a compelling image and a fast, intuitive story.
